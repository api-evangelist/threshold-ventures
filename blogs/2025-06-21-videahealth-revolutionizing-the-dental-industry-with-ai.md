By Emily Melton and Lisa Xu We are excited to lead the $40 million Series B fundraising round for VideaHealth, an AI company revolutionizing dentistry and redefining how dental practices deliver care. Like many markets we invest in, the dental industry is on the precipice of a massive transformation, accelerated by AI and driven by long-term market forces, including the rise of dental service organizations and a generational shift in the dental workforce. We are confident that AI will inevitably and fundamentally change the dental ecosystem and that Videa will lead the charge for AI adoption.
